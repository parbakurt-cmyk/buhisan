Welcome to Buhisan Elementary and Night High School

Located in the heart of Cebu City, Philippines, Buhisan Elementary and Night High School has been a cornerstone of quality education since 2016. We are committed to nurturing young minds and shaping future leaders through excellence in academics, character development, and community engagement. Our institution serves as a beacon of hope and opportunity for students from diverse backgrounds, providing them with the tools and knowledge they need to succeed in an ever-changing world.

Elementary Department (Grades 1-6)

Our Elementary Department provides a strong foundation for young learners, focusing on holistic development that encompasses academic excellence, social skills, and emotional intelligence. With dedicated and experienced teachers, we create a nurturing environment where children feel safe, valued, and inspired to explore their potential. Our curriculum is designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and a genuine love for learning through interactive lessons, hands-on activities, and collaborative projects.

We emphasize core subjects including Mathematics, Science, English, Filipino, and Araling Panlipunan, while also integrating values education, arts, music, and physical education to ensure well-rounded development. Our small class sizes allow for personalized attention, ensuring that each child receives the support they need to thrive academically and socially. Through innovative teaching methods and a student-centered approach, we prepare our elementary students to become confident, compassionate, and capable individuals ready to face the challenges of higher education.

Junior High School Department (Grades 7-10)

The Night High School program serves as a testament to our commitment to inclusive and accessible education. Recognizing that many students and working individuals face barriers to traditional daytime schooling, we offer evening classes that accommodate their unique circumstances. Our Junior High School curriculum covers Grades 7 through 10, providing comprehensive instruction in core academic subjects including Advanced Mathematics, Sciences, English, Filipino, Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE), MAPEH, and values formation.

Our experienced faculty members are dedicated to creating an engaging and supportive learning environment, even during evening hours. We utilize modern teaching methodologies and resources to ensure that our night high school students receive the same quality education as their daytime counterparts. The program is structured to help students develop critical thinking skills, technological literacy, and practical life skills that will serve them well in their future careers and personal endeavors. Whether students are working during the day, helping their families, or pursuing other responsibilities, our flexible schedule empowers them to continue their education without compromise.

At Buhisan Elementary and Night High School, we believe that education is the key to unlocking potential and creating brighter futures. Join our community of learners, where dreams are nurtured, talents are discovered, and success stories are written every day.
